The folder contains .csv files with data columns.

Each column information: Year 2022, Day 15, Month: March, June, September, December

Hourly Data: Transmittance of 737 nm wavelength in the atmospheric channel,

Calculation : 

T = Transmittance assuming zero cloud cover and maximum visibility; irrespective of specific year
CC = Hourly cloud cover percentage of the year 2022

*Final transmittance  = T * (1- (CC/100))

*This is the value listed in .csv files of this zip folder.
